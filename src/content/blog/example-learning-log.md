## Question

What makes a useful technical learning note? A good starting point is to keep the question, the attempted method, and the result close together.

### Template

```text
Question: What am I trying to understand?
Method: What did I read, build, or test?
Result: What changed in my understanding?
```

### Checklist

- [ ] State the problem precisely.
- [ ] Save the key references.
- [ ] Write the next question.
